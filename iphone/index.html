Pets OS for Stephone

On iPhone · Version 1.3.0 · Free

A new operating system created by Fionn Mpho Mwaba · petsos.shouldbe.media

The same Pets OS as on Android: Rest, Clarice, Pen, Moments, Weather, Calendar, Clock, Calculator, Activities, O Store (get Milo, the Pets OS web browser, there) and Settings, with the Pets OS lock screen and passcode. Stephone, the Pets OS phone, is coming soon.

Open Pets OS

How to add it to your iPhone

  1. On your iPhone, open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Pets OS.
  3. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Pets OS from its new Home Screen icon. It runs full screen and works offline.

Good to know

  • Apple doesn't let any app replace the iPhone's own Home Screen or lock screen, so on iPhone Pets OS opens from its icon like an app.
  • Your notes, photos and settings stay on your iPhone. Weather and web searches use the internet.
  • iPhone only lets its own apps set alarms and use the torch, so those live in Apple's Clock and Control Centre.
  • Updates arrive by themselves the next time you open Pets OS with an internet connection.

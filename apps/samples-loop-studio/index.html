Samples Loop Studio

A native music studio for macOS.

A native music studio for Mac with an FL Studio–style workflow: playlist arrangement, channel-rack step sequencer, piano roll, mixer with EQ and sidechain compression, tempo automation, MIDI and typing-keyboard input, note recording and floating editor windows.

Install

  1. Click Download. Safari unzips it automatically; in other browsers, double-click the .zip.
  2. Drag the app into your Applications folder.
  3. Open the app. macOS says Apple could not verify it — click Done (not Move to Trash).
  4. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and click Open Anyway next to the app's name. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  5. Open the app again and click Open. You only need to do this once.

Known limitations

  • Developer Preview: ad-hoc signed and not yet notarized by Apple (no Developer ID certificate yet), so macOS asks you to approve it once.
  • Needs a screen at least 1440 points wide.
  • Microphone recording asks for permission.

Verify your download

Check the file is authentic: in Terminal, run shasum -a 256 followed by the file's name, and compare the result with the SHA-256 below.
